  • ✅ Always support your answers with reasons and examples
  • ✅ Use a wide range of vocabulary and grammar structures
  • ✅ Keep talking — silence costs marks
  • ✅ Listen and respond to your partner actively
  • ✅ It's okay to self-correct; it shows awareness
  • ❌ Don't just describe — compare, speculate and evaluate
  • ❌ Don't dominate in Parts 3 & 4 — invite your partner
  • ❌ Don't memorise scripted answers — they sound unnatural
  • ❌ Don't rush — pace yourself and be clear
  • ❌ Don't panic about occasional errors — fluency matters more
